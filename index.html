Prepare for Your Study Journey in the United States

A practical guide for international students preparing for admission, I-20, F-1 visa, arrival, and campus life in the U.S.

Key Steps for International Students

1. Apply for Admission

Prepare your application, transcripts, English proficiency documents, passport copy, and financial support documents.

2. Receive Your I-20

After admission and financial document review, eligible F-1 students receive a Form I-20 from the school.

3. Pay SEVIS I-901 Fee

Students use the SEVIS ID on the I-20 to pay the required I-901 fee before the visa interview.

4. Apply for F-1 Visa

Schedule a visa interview, prepare supporting documents, and explain your study plan clearly.

Pre-Arrival Checklist

Travel Documents

Keep your passport, F-1 visa, I-20, admission letter, and financial documents in your carry-on bag when traveling.

Housing Plan

Confirm your housing arrangement before arrival and understand your move-in date, address, and emergency contact information.

Arrival Communication

Contact your international student office after arrival and attend required orientation sessions.

Academic Preparation

Review your class schedule, placement requirements, academic advisor information, and campus technology accounts.

Frequently Asked Questions

When can I enter the United States?

New F-1 students should carefully review the program start date and entry rules listed on their immigration documents.

Can I work while studying?

F-1 students have specific employment rules. Always speak with your DSO before starting any employment.
